## 1 · The chain

Every claim the system makes sits at one of three positions:

- **Origin** — the cause, the event, the source. *Never directly observable.*
- **Relationship** — the response, the return, the felt gap. *The only directly
  observable position.*
- **Identity** — what something or someone *is*. *Never observable; only inferable.*

## 2 · The inversion

The runtime computes forward: event → response, text → panel.
The doctrine measures backward: response → event, flow → pressure, trace → origin.
**Both are specified; only the forward direction is wired.**

The week's evidence in ORI terms: the open sensor port (P/M/T/D/E arrive as given
floats — `v2_Individual_Support.txt:432-446`), the missing return loop (the "you"
axis of the 2×2), the orphaned `runtime_continuity`, the absent HOC layer, geometry
canonized as unimplemented (`docs/geometry_system.md:9`). The architecture sits at
Origin; the substrate sits at Relationship; the gap between them is the unwired
module inventory.

## 3 · The reflection rule

*The observer appears at Origin only as reflection.* Tracing a response backward
reconstructs the system's own model of the cause — and the failure class of the week
is **mistaking that reflection for the source**: `role_confusion`, "orphaned or
resentful", τ-as-knob, "the panel is unresponsive". Operational form:

> Any claim positioned at Origin must carry its reconstruction path — verb, object,
> antecedent. No path → tagged `reflection`, never `finding`.

This is the two-tier residue audit re-anchored: tier 1 checks the claim has parts
(verb–object–traits); tier 2 checks the parts trace to Relationship-layer evidence
(D8 antecedent pass).

## 4 · The identity rule

Identity claims are inferences from *repeated* Relationship-layer observations, never
single events. Canonical form (PATTERN EVALUATION PROTOCOL v1.0, line 79):

> "Never infer intent. Infer what was treated as costly, from what was repeatedly
> not chosen."

One observation licenses nothing at Identity position.

## 5 · The relationship rule

All legitimate measurement channels live at Relationship — intake declarations, track
record, telemetry — because it is the only position where measurement is possible.
Every legal magnitude source in the transfer-options analysis was a
Relationship-layer instrument; the illegal one (`len(matches)` → float) tried to
manufacture Relationship-layer evidence from kind-layer text.
